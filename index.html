Never miss another roofing lead.

Field Call Works is an AI phone receptionist for roofing and home-service companies. It answers every call, day or night, collects the caller's details, and texts the lead to your team in seconds.

Get in touch

What you get

Every call answered

After hours, on the roof, or during a storm rush, callers reach a friendly receptionist instead of voicemail.

Qualified leads

Name, callback number, property address, the issue, and the best time to call back, confirmed with the caller.

Instant text alerts

Your team gets each new lead by text right away, and every lead is saved to a shared sheet.

Caller confirmations

Callers who agree to texts get a confirmation, so they know you have their request.

How it works

  1. A homeowner calls your business number.
  2. The receptionist answers, tells the caller the call may be recorded, and asks about the roofing issue.
  3. It collects the caller's details and reads them back to confirm.
  4. It asks whether the caller wants text updates. Texts are optional, and callers who say no are still helped.
  5. Your team receives the lead by text and calls the homeowner back.
